Privacy note

This is a plain-language summary of how Joystick (“we”) handles personal information under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). It is a general note for this placeholder website — not legal advice.

Who we are

Joystick is an adult lifestyle shop based in Durban, South Africa. Contact details will be published here once the business channels (WhatsApp / email) are confirmed.

What we collect

  • Order details you send us (name, delivery address, phone, WhatsApp number, items ordered).
  • Payment references you share for EFT / Instant EFT.
  • Basic technical data stored on your device (for example, an 18+ age confirmation and bag contents in local storage). That data stays on your device unless you send an order to us.

Why we use it

  • To confirm and fulfil orders.
  • To arrange discreet shipping and answer your messages.
  • To keep records required for accounting and consumer law.

Sharing

We only share what is needed to deliver your order (for example, a courier) or when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Security & discretion

Orders ship in plain packaging. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure. Please do not send ID documents or card numbers over WhatsApp.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to certain processing, subject to the law. To do that, message us on the contact channel published on the site.

Children

This site is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this note as the business grows. The latest version will always be on this page.
